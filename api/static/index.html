    Explore the data
    
        
    Simple measurements taken from each of the 2,577 original photos, and what they mean for the model.
    
      
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Rainbows are rare in the data
    
          
    Fog/smog has about 3.7 times as many photos as rainbow. A model trained on this as-is would lean towards the common classes, so training gives rare classes more weight and the model is judged on macro F1, which counts every class equally. The paler bars show the training set today, including images added through retraining.
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Lightning is dark, fog is bright, and rain and hail look alike
    
          
    Lightning photos are by far the darkest because most are taken at night. Fog/smog is the brightest because haze scatters light. Rain and hail are almost identical in both brightness and contrast, so these numbers alone cannot separate them. The model has to learn texture instead, and this is where most of its mistakes happen.
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Colour separates fog, lightning and rainbow
    
          
    In fog/smog photos red, green and blue are almost equal, which is what grey looks like in numbers, and saturation is the lowest. Lightning has the strongest colour, a blue and purple glow at night. Rainbow photos are the bluest overall because they are shot against open sky. Since colour carries this much signal, training never shifts colours when it augments images.
